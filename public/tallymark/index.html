How Tallymark works.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded and there is no account.

  1. Print the sheets

    Choose Fluency sheet, then print the student copy and the teacher copy.

  2. Start the timer

    Press Timer and score, then Start. Say go, and the child reads across each row.

  3. Mark the teacher copy

    Slash each miss. When the chime sounds, draw a line after the last item reached.

  4. Save the score

    Enter reached and errors. Tallymark works out the rest and adds it to the chart.

How the score works

The child reached 11 letters before time ran out and missed 2. The count at the end of each row on the teacher copy makes the total easy to find.

11 reached − 2 errors = 9 correct

To save scores, add a class and its students on the Classes tab first.

Good to know

The weekly scramble

The week's Monday date decides the order, so the same week always gives the same sheet and next week brings a new one. For your own order, choose My own code and type any words.

Saved setups

Pick a format, sets and options once, then type a name under Saved setups and press Save. Press the name any time to bring them back.

Printing and A4

Choose US Letter or A4 under Printing. In the print dialog, use 100% scale and turn off headers and footers. If a browser prints oddly, press Save PDF and print the file.

Your data and backups

Classes, scores and setups live only in this browser, so they do not follow you to another device. Press Download backup on the Progress tab now and then. Tallymark reminds you after 30 days.

Offline and install

Open the site once, then use your browser's Install option. It works without internet after that. On an iPhone or iPad, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Cost and accounts

It is free and needs no account. Nothing is uploaded, so there is nothing for anyone else to see.

Privacy. Class lists, scores and setups stay on this device. Nothing is uploaded, and there are no accounts or analytics.
